# XP Potion x Rascals: Powering the Next Generation of Esports

A sponsorship built on community, authenticity, and shared passion for gaming culture.

## A New Chapter for XP Potion

From the very beginning, XP Potion has been more than a drink — it’s been about building a world where gamers feel empowered, connected, and supported.

Today, we’re taking that mission to the next level with our first official esports partnership: XP Potion x Rascals.

The Rascals aren’t just another team.

They’re fierce competitors, fearless creators, and unapologetic gamers who live and breathe the culture — the exact qualities that define XP Potion.

Together, we’re not just putting logos on jerseys; we’re creating a movement that blends performance, community, and authentic gaming energy.

## Why Rascals?

Esports is more than a spectator sport — it’s a lifestyle.

And few teams embody that better than the Rascals.

Known for their bold playstyle, tight-knit chemistry, and vibrant personalities, Rascals represent everything XP Potion stands for:

- Empowerment — pushing limits, levelling up, and proving what’s possible.

- Authenticity — competing and streaming with unfiltered passion, no gimmicks.

- Community — connecting with fans not as followers, but as teammates.

- Innovation — bringing fresh energy to every match, every stream, every event.

- Balance — proving that high performance and fun can (and should) go hand in hand.

In short: the Rascals play the game the way we believe energy should be lived — with intensity, creativity, and respect for the grind.

## More Than a Sponsorship

This isn’t about slapping our logo on a jersey and calling it a day.

XP Potion and Rascals are joining forces to co-create content, community events, and experiences that bring gamers together both online and offline.

Expect to see:

- Exclusive behind-the-scenes content with the Rascals powered by XP Potion.

- Co-branded livestreams and tournaments where fans can squad up with their favourite players.

- Community challenges, giveaways, and digital drops designed to keep the XP flowing.

- Meet-ups and events where fans and players can connect, share, and celebrate gaming culture.

When we say community-first, we mean it!

This partnership is as much about the players and fans as it is about the brands.

The Rascals aren’t just sipping XP Potion — they’re living it.

As our official brand ambassadors, they’ll share their experiences, routines, and stories with our community.

## Building the Future Together

At XP Potion, we believe the future of gaming belongs to those who play boldly, collaborate authentically, and share their journey with others.

By teaming up with the Rascals, we’re doubling down on our commitment to empowerment, authenticity, and community.

This partnership is just the beginning.

Together, XP Potion and Rascals will be launching new initiatives designed to inspire the next generation of gamers, creators, and competitors.
